## Summary

Dr. Heather Robinson, ND, sees patients across Scotland by secure video, so you can have a full naturopathic consultation from Edinburgh, Glasgow, St Andrews or anywhere between. She is a full member of the Association of Naturopathic Practitioners (UK).

## Overview

Scotland is well suited to virtual care. Distances are long, and many people split their week between the Central Belt, the coast and the Highlands. A video consultation removes the travel, and all testing and follow-ups can be arranged around your calendar. Whether you are in Edinburgh’s New Town, Glasgow’s West End or a town on the east coast, the appointment is the same: a 75-minute conversation about your health history, then a plan built around you.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can I see a naturopath online in Scotland?

Yes. Dr. Heather Robinson, ND, sees patients across Scotland by secure video. You do not need to travel, and your first step is a free 15-minute discovery call.

### Do you offer appointments in Edinburgh or Glasgow in person?

No. Village Naturopathy is a virtual clinic, so all consultations take place online. That means the same care is available whether you live in a city, a town or a rural area.

### How much does a naturopathic consultation cost in Scotland?

The initial 75-minute consultation is £200 and follow-ups are £50–170 depending on length. The 15-minute discovery call is free.
