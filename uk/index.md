## Frequently asked questions

### Can I see a naturopath online in the UK?

Yes. Dr. Heather Robinson, ND, sees patients across England, Scotland, Wales and Northern Ireland by secure video. Start with a free 15-minute discovery call, then book a 75-minute initial consultation.

### Is naturopathy regulated in the UK?

Naturopathy is not statutorily regulated in the UK. Dr. Heather is a full member of the Association of Naturopathic Practitioners (UK), is registered with the College of Naturopaths of Ontario, where naturopathic doctors are regulated, and is fully insured.

### How much does a naturopath cost in the UK?

At Village Naturopathy the initial 75-minute consultation is £200 and follow-ups are £50–170 depending on length. The 15-minute discovery call is free. Testing, if recommended, is discussed with you first.

### What can a naturopath help with?

Dr. Heather focuses on hormones (PMS, PMOS/PCOS, endometriosis, irregular periods, hormonal acne), stress, mood and anxiety, fatigue and gut health (IBS, SIBO, food sensitivities). Care is complementary and works alongside your GP.

### Do you accept private medical insurance?

Receipts are provided for every appointment, but whether a private health insurer reimburses naturopathy depends on your policy, so please check with your insurer first.

### Do I need to travel anywhere?

No. Village Naturopathy is a virtual clinic. Consultations and follow-ups take place by secure video, and any recommended testing is explained to you first.
