## Summary

Village Naturopathy offers online naturopathic care for burnout, chronic stress, anxiety and low mood for people in London. Dr. Heather Robinson, ND, looks at your nervous system, sleep, hormones, gut and nutrient status, then builds a personalised plan that fits a demanding London schedule.

## When stress becomes your baseline

London rewards people who keep going. After enough years of early starts, late evenings and constant availability, being tired, tense and slightly on edge starts to feel normal. But persistent stress affects sleep, digestion, hormones and mood, and those effects can be investigated and supported.

## How we approach burnout and stress

Dr. Heather combines a thorough history with the physical contributors: blood sugar, nutrient status, cortisol patterns, thyroid function and gut health. The plan usually includes nutrition, targeted supplementation, botanical medicine, nervous system regulation techniques and realistic changes to your routine. She is also a Certified HeartMath Practitioner and integrates those techniques where they are useful.

## Working with your GP

Naturopathic care is complementary. If you are also seeing a GP or therapist, Dr. Heather is happy to work alongside them. If your mood is very low or you are struggling to cope, please contact your GP or a crisis service as well.
