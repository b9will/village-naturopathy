## What hormone-focused naturopathic care looks like

Hormone symptoms rarely have a single cause. Cycles are influenced by stress, sleep, blood sugar, thyroid function, gut health, nutrient status and the way your body clears hormones. A naturopathic assessment looks at those together instead of treating each symptom in isolation. Dr. Heather starts with a detailed history: your cycle, symptoms, contraceptive history, stress load and goals. From there she recommends testing only where it will change the plan, and builds a programme of nutrition, targeted supplements, botanical medicine and lifestyle support.

## Common reasons London patients get in touch

PMS and PMDD that disrupt work and home life, irregular, heavy or painful periods, PMOS/PCOS, endometriosis, hormonal acne, post-pill syndrome and fertility support. Many arrive after being told their standard blood results are fine, and still know that something is off. The plan is personalised, so a cycle-tracking approach for one person may look quite different from the approach for someone else, even if the label on the symptoms is the same.

## Testing, if it helps

Comprehensive blood work and DUTCH hormone testing can give a clearer picture of sex hormones, cortisol patterns and hormone metabolites. Testing is never required to start care. Dr. Heather recommends it case by case and explains the cost and logistics first.
