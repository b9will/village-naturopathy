Common questions

Frequently asked questions

Can I see a naturopath online in Dulwich?

Yes. Dr. Heather Robinson, ND, sees patients in Dulwich and across the UK by secure video. Start with a free 15-minute discovery call, then book a 75-minute initial consultation if you would like to go ahead.

Do you have a clinic in Dulwich?

No. Village Naturopathy is a virtual clinic and all consultations take place online. Dulwich patients receive exactly the same care as patients anywhere else in the UK.

How much does a naturopath cost in Dulwich?

The initial 75-minute consultation is £200 and follow-ups are £50–170 depending on length. The 15-minute discovery call is free. Any testing is optional and discussed with you before you decide.

Which postcodes do you cover near Dulwich?

Because care is online, there are no postcode limits. This page is written for SE21 and SE22, but patients from across London and the rest of the UK are welcome.
