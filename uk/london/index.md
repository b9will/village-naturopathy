## Naturopathic care for life in London

London asks a lot of your body. Long commutes, back-to-back meetings, late dinners, little daylight in winter and a social calendar that never quite switches off all leave their mark on sleep, digestion and hormones. If you have been told your blood tests are ‘normal’ while you still feel exhausted, wired at night or unwell around your cycle, you are not imagining it. Virtual care fits a London week. There is no clinic to get across the city to, and appointments can sit around a working day or a school run. Dr. Heather’s approach is root-cause and personalised: a full history, comprehensive testing where it is useful, and a plan you can realistically follow.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can I see a naturopath online in London?

Yes. Dr. Heather Robinson, ND, sees patients across London by secure video. There is no need to travel to a clinic. Start with a free 15-minute discovery call, then book a 75-minute initial consultation if you would like to go ahead.

### Is Dr. Heather based in London?

No. Village Naturopathy is a virtual clinic, and every consultation takes place online. London patients receive the same care as patients anywhere in the UK.

### How much does a naturopath cost in London?

The initial 75-minute consultation is £200 and follow-up appointments are £50–170 depending on length. The 15-minute discovery call is free. Any testing is optional and discussed with you before you decide.

### Do you offer DUTCH hormone testing for patients in London?

Where DUTCH testing is useful, Dr. Heather explains how it works, what it measures and the cost before you decide. It is optional and recommended case by case.

### Is naturopathy regulated in the UK?

Naturopathy is not statutorily regulated in the UK. Dr. Heather is a full member of the Association of Naturopathic Practitioners (UK) and is registered with the College of Naturopaths of Ontario, where naturopathic doctors are regulated. She is fully insured.
