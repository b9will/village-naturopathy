## What the DUTCH test is

DUTCH stands for Dried Urine Test for Comprehensive Hormones. You collect small dried urine samples at home on filter paper, usually four samples across a day, and send them to the laboratory. It measures sex hormones such as oestrogen, progesterone and testosterone, plus their metabolites, and the daily pattern of cortisol. Some versions of the test also look at markers linked to melatonin, oxidative stress, and certain B vitamins. A DUTCH report adds detail to your history. It does not diagnose a condition on its own, and results are always interpreted alongside your symptoms and other tests.

## When it can be useful

Dr. Heather may recommend DUTCH testing when cycles are irregular or difficult, when symptoms suggest a hormone pattern that blood tests do not explain, when you are coming off hormonal contraception, or when cortisol rhythm looks relevant to sleep and energy. It is never required to start care.
