How it works

How online naturopathic care works

1. A free 15-minute discovery call

A short, no-pressure conversation to hear what is going on and check we are a good fit. Book your call.

2. A 75-minute consultation by secure video

A thorough look at your history, symptoms, cycle, sleep, stress and nutrition. You can attend from home or work, anywhere in the UK.

3. A personalised plan and follow-ups

Nutrition, targeted supplementation, botanical medicine and lifestyle support, with comprehensive testing recommended only where it will change the plan.
