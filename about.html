About Dr. Heather

Trained in both psychology and naturopathic medicine — because your mind and body aren’t separate.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

What is a naturopath and what is naturopathic medicine?

Naturopathic medicine is a distinct system of primary care that uses natural therapeutics to identify and treat the root cause of illness. It combines modern science with traditional healing methods including clinical nutrition, herbal medicine, and lifestyle counselling. Naturopathy is complementary to, not a replacement for, conventional medical care.

Can I see a naturopath online in the UK?

Yes. Dr. Robinson is a full member of the Association of Naturopathic Practitioners (UK) and offers UK-wide virtual care, as well as appointments for patients in Ontario. Naturopathy is not statutorily regulated in the UK. Dr. Robinson is also registered with the College of Naturopaths of Ontario. See UK locations.

How do online naturopathic consultations work?

All consultations are delivered via secure video call. You’ll receive a link before your appointment, and all you need is a quiet space and a reliable internet connection. Your initial consultation is 75 minutes and includes a complete health assessment and personalised treatment plan, with possible lab requisitions. A complimentary 15-minute discovery call is available first to make sure we’re the right fit.

What conditions does Village Naturopathy treat?

Village Naturopathy focuses on hormone balancing (PMOS/PCOS, PMS, endometriosis, irregular cycles, acne), stress, mood and anxiety, fatigue and energy, and gut health (IBS, SIBO, food sensitivities).

What diagnostic testing do you offer?

Village Naturopathy offers comprehensive blood work, DUTCH hormone testing, GI-MAP digestive analysis, SIBO breath testing, organic acid testing (OAT), mycotoxin panels and hair mineral analysis. Testing is recommended based on individual needs.
