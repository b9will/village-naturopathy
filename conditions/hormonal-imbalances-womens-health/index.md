Painful periods, unexplained weight gain, acne that won’t quit, mood swings that disrupt your relationships: these aren’t things you should just live with. Hormonal imbalances have identifiable causes and treatable solutions. We dig into the data and create a plan that actually works.

## How we work with you

- Detailed Intake: Your menstrual history, symptom patterns, contraceptive history, and health goals.
- Hormone Mapping: DUTCH complete panels, thyroid function, metabolic markers, and blood work for a full hormonal picture. Testing isn’t required to start care. Any testing is recommended case by case, based on your individual needs.
- Personalised Plan: Clinical nutrition, evidence-based botanical medicine and targeted supplementation to restore balance.
- Cycle-Tracked Follow-ups: Progress monitoring aligned with your menstrual cycle for precise adjustments.

## Frequently asked questions

### What hormonal conditions do you treat?

We treat PMOS/PCOS, endometriosis, PMS/PMDD, irregular periods, hormonal acne, fertility challenges, post-pill syndrome, and more.

### How is PMOS/PCOS treated naturopathically?

We focus on the root drivers: insulin resistance, inflammation, and androgen excess. Treatment includes dietary changes, targeted supplementation, botanical medicine, and stress management.

### Can you help with fertility?

Yes. We optimise hormonal health, address underlying conditions, and support conception through evidence-based naturopathic protocols. We work alongside fertility specialists when needed.
