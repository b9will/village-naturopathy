## Frequently asked questions

### What is Hashimoto's thyroiditis?

Hashimoto’s is an autoimmune condition where the immune system attacks the thyroid gland, gradually reducing its function. It’s the most common cause of hypothyroidism and is often underdiagnosed.

### Can naturopathy help autoimmune conditions?

While we can’t cure autoimmune disease, naturopathic medicine can support the body by calming inflammation, healing the gut (a major driver of autoimmunity), and improving quality of life. It works alongside your medical care.

### What is mould illness?

Chronic Inflammatory Response Syndrome (CIRS) from mould exposure can cause fatigue, brain fog, joint pain, and respiratory issues. We test for mycotoxins and provide targeted support.
