## Frequently asked questions

### How is acne related to gut health?

The gut-skin axis is well-established. Gut inflammation, bacterial imbalances, and intestinal permeability can trigger systemic inflammation that may show up on the skin.

### Can hormones cause adult acne?

Yes. Shifts in androgens, insulin and cortisol are common drivers of adult and hormonal acne, particularly along the jawline and around your cycle. Testing can help clarify what is happening for you.

### Is acne connected to PMOS/PCOS?

It can be. PMOS (Polyendocrine Metabolic Ovarian Syndrome, also known as PCOS) often involves raised androgens, which may contribute to acne. A full cycle and symptom history is the starting point.
