## Frequently asked questions

### Why am I always tired even though my blood work is normal?

Standard blood work often checks only a few markers, and ‘normal’ ranges are broad. We look more closely at nutrient status, thyroid function, hormones and cortisol, alongside your sleep, stress and lifestyle, to find what’s draining your energy.

### What tests do you use to investigate fatigue?

Usually comprehensive blood work, including iron, B12, vitamin D, thyroid markers and hormones. More in-depth testing is only recommended if it’s needed for you.

### Can low iron or B12 cause fatigue?

Yes. Iron and B12 deficiencies are among the most common and most overlooked causes of fatigue, especially for women with heavy periods, plant-based diets or digestive issues.

### Can a naturopath help with burnout?

Yes. We look at how long-term stress has affected your sleep, hormones, nervous system and nutrient levels, then build a plan to help you rebuild your energy step by step.

### How long does it take to get my energy back?

It depends on what’s driving your fatigue. Many patients notice changes within 4–8 weeks, with a full plan typically running 3–6 months.
