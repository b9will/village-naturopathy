## Frequently asked questions

### What is DUTCH testing?

DUTCH (Dried Urine Test for Comprehensive Hormones) measures your sex hormones, cortisol pattern, and hormone metabolites. It gives us a far more detailed picture than standard blood work alone.

### Can naturopathy help with PMOS/PCOS?

Yes. PMOS (Polyendocrine Metabolic Ovarian Syndrome) is the updated name for PCOS. Naturopathic treatment focuses on insulin regulation, reducing inflammation and supporting hormone balance through nutrition, botanicals and lifestyle changes, addressing the root drivers rather than just the symptoms.

### Can a naturopath help with irregular or painful periods?

Yes. We look at what’s driving your cycle changes, from nutrient status and blood sugar to stress and inflammation, and build a plan to support a healthier, more comfortable cycle.

### Can naturopathy help with PMS or PMDD?

Yes. We assess how your hormones, nervous system and nutrition interact across your cycle, then use targeted nutrition, supplementation and botanicals to reduce symptoms month to month.

### Do you offer online hormone consultations in the UK?

Yes. All appointments are virtual, and Dr. Heather sees patients across the UK and Ontario.

### How long does hormone balancing take?

Hormonal shifts typically take 3–6 menstrual cycles to fully manifest. Most patients notice improvements in energy and mood within the first month.

### Do I need to stop birth control?

Not necessarily. We work with wherever you are. If you’re considering coming off hormonal contraception, we can support that transition.
