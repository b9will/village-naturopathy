## Frequently asked questions

### What causes chronic stress and anxiety?

Chronic stress is rarely caused by one thing. It’s usually a combination of lifestyle factors, unresolved trauma, hormonal imbalances, nutrient deficiencies, and nervous system dysregulation. Naturopathic medicine looks at all of these together.

### How does naturopathy treat anxiety differently?

Rather than masking symptoms, we identify root causes, whether that’s a cortisol imbalance, gut-brain axis disruption or a mineral deficiency, and address them with targeted nutrition, botanicals and nervous system tools.

### Can a naturopath help with anxiety?

A naturopathic doctor can look at the physical contributors to anxiety, such as blood sugar swings, nutrient status, hormones, sleep and gut health, and support them alongside the lifestyle and nervous system factors. Appointments are virtual, for patients across the UK and Ontario. Naturopathic care is complementary, so we work alongside your GP or mental health team.

### What is the best natural treatment for stress and anxiety?

There is no single best option, because stress looks different in every person. A plan usually combines nutrition, targeted supplementation, botanical medicine, sleep support and nervous system regulation practices, chosen after a full history and, where useful, testing. Dr. Heather approves the plan with you, step by step.

### Can naturopathy help with PMDD or low mood?

Naturopathic care can support the hormonal, nutritional and nervous system factors that often sit behind PMDD and low mood, and it works alongside medical care. Virtual consultations are available in the UK and Ontario. If you are struggling with thoughts of harming yourself, please contact your GP or emergency services straight away, or call Samaritans on 116 123 (UK) or 988 (Canada).

### How long does treatment typically take?

Most patients notice improvements within 4–6 weeks. A comprehensive treatment plan typically spans 3–6 months, with follow-ups adjusted to your pace.

### Do you work alongside my GP or therapist?

Absolutely. Naturopathic care is integrative, and we collaborate with your existing healthcare team to ensure a cohesive approach.
