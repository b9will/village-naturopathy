## Frequently asked questions

### What is a GI-MAP test and do I need one?

GI-MAP is a comprehensive stool test that uses DNA analysis to identify pathogens, parasites, bacterial imbalances, inflammation markers, and digestive function. It’s one of the most detailed gut tests available. Not everyone needs testing. We recommend it based on your symptoms and history.

### How long does it take to heal your gut naturally?

It depends on the condition. Simple dysbiosis may resolve in 6–8 weeks. More complex issues like SIBO or inflammatory conditions typically take 3–6 months of structured treatment.

### Do I need to follow a restrictive diet?

Short-term dietary modifications are sometimes helpful, but the goal is always to expand your diet, not restrict it permanently. We focus on healing the gut so you can enjoy food again.

### Can gut health affect my mood?

Absolutely. The gut-brain axis is a well-established connection. Around 90% of the body’s serotonin is produced in the gut, and gut inflammation directly impacts mood, anxiety, and cognitive function.

### Can a naturopath help with IBS or SIBO?

Yes. We look for what’s driving your symptoms, whether that’s bacterial overgrowth, low stomach acid, stress or food triggers, and build a targeted plan using nutrition, botanicals and nutraceuticals. SIBO breath testing is available when it’s useful.

### Why am I bloated after every meal?

Persistent bloating can come from several causes, including SIBO, food intolerances, low digestive enzymes or stress affecting digestion. An assessment helps identify which ones apply to you.
