Conditions treated

Root-cause naturopathic care for the conditions holding you back.

We treat the whole person, not just the diagnosis. Here are the most common conditions we work with, for patients across the UK and Ontario.

Stress & nervous system

Stress, Anxiety & Nervous System Dysregulation

Chronic stress isn’t just mental. It disrupts sleep, digestion, hormones, immunity, and energy. We use evidence-based naturopathic strategies to calm the nervous system, rebuild resilience, and address the physical fallout of long-term stress.

  • Chronic stress & overwhelm
  • Generalised anxiety disorder
  • Panic attacks
  • Burnout & adrenal fatigue
  • Insomnia & sleep disturbances
  • Nervous system dysregulation
  • Depression & low mood
  • PMDD

Hormones

Hormonal Imbalances & Women’s Health

Hormone issues are common but not normal. From painful periods to PMS and PMOS/PCOS, we use comprehensive testing and root-cause treatment to bring your hormones back into balance without masking symptoms.

  • PMOS/PCOS (Polyendocrine Metabolic Ovarian Syndrome)
  • PMS & PMDD
  • Heavy or painful periods
  • Endometriosis
  • Irregular or absent periods
  • Hormonal acne
  • Fertility support
  • Post-pill syndrome
Request a consultation Learn more

Gut health

Digestive & Gut Health Conditions

A healthy gut is the foundation of overall health, affecting your hormones, mood, immunity, and energy. We use functional testing and targeted protocols to get your gut working properly again.

  • IBS (Irritable Bowel Syndrome)
  • SIBO (Small Intestinal Bacterial Overgrowth)
  • Bloating & gas
  • Constipation & diarrhoea
  • Food sensitivities & intolerances
  • Acid reflux & GERD
  • Leaky gut (intestinal permeability)
  • Candida overgrowth
  • Inflammatory bowel conditions

Energy & fatigue

Fatigue, Sleep & Energy Conditions

If you’re tired all the time, running on coffee, or waking up unrefreshed, there’s a reason. We investigate the root causes of fatigue and build a plan to restore sustainable energy.

  • Chronic fatigue
  • Adrenal dysfunction
  • Poor sleep quality
  • Difficulty waking or staying asleep
  • Low energy & brain fog
  • Iron deficiency & anaemia
  • Thyroid-related fatigue

Thyroid & autoimmune

Thyroid & Autoimmune Conditions

Thyroid and autoimmune conditions are complex and deeply personal. We use comprehensive testing to understand what’s driving your symptoms and build plans that support the underlying immune and metabolic picture.

  • Hypothyroidism
  • Hashimoto’s thyroiditis
  • Hyperthyroidism & Graves’ disease
  • Autoimmune conditions
  • Chronic inflammation
  • Mould illness & mycotoxin exposure

Acne & skin

Acne & Hormonal Skin Health

Persistent acne often reflects what’s happening inside: hormones, blood sugar, the gut and stress. We look for the internal drivers rather than only suppressing symptoms.

  • Hormonal acne
  • Adult acne
  • Acne linked to PMOS/PCOS
  • Acne linked to gut health or stress
  • Hair loss
